# BEYOND ORBIT
### Abandoned but Not Forgotten: The Untold Stories of NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars

**NASA Space Apps Challenge**

## About the Project

Beyond Orbit is an educational storytelling project developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge, based on Theme 1: **"Abandoned but Not Forgotten."**

The project explores the history of abandoned, discontinued, and forgotten equipment and technological projects associated with space exploration on the Moon and Mars.

Throughout the history of space exploration, numerous technologies have been developed, tested, and sent beyond Earth. Some successfully fulfilled their missions, while others were abandoned, discontinued, or left behind due to technical challenges and changing mission objectives.

Beyond Orbit aims to bring these stories back to life, highlighting their historical significance and the scientific knowledge they represent.

## Project Objectives

- **Preserve Space History:** Document and showcase abandoned equipment and discontinued projects related to the Moon and Mars.
- **Promote Education:** Make historical and scientific information accessible through digital storytelling.
- **Raise Awareness:** Highlight the importance of unsuccessful missions and abandoned technologies in the advancement of space exploration.
- **Celebrate Innovation:** Recognize the engineering, research, and challenges behind humanity's exploration of other worlds.

## Why Beyond Orbit?

Space exploration is not defined only by successful missions, historic discoveries, or technological breakthroughs.

It is also shaped by experiments that failed, machines that were left behind, and projects that never achieved their original objectives.

Every abandoned piece of equipment represents a chapter in the history of scientific exploration. Every discontinued project carries lessons that contribute to future discoveries.

Beyond Orbit exists to ensure that these stories remain part of our collective memory.

## About NASA Space Apps Challenge

The NASA Space Apps Challenge is an international hackathon that brings together people from different backgrounds to solve challenges using NASA's open data and resources.

Beyond Orbit was developed as our contribution to this global initiative, exploring the forgotten side of humanity's journey beyond Earth.
